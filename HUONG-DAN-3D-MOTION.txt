AI PROJECT OS v0.9.60 - BAN VA IDEA STUDIO 3D
=============================================

DAY LA BAN VA MA NGUON, KHONG PHAI BO CAI EXE WINDOWS MOI.

Cach dung tren may da cai AI Project OS v0.9.60:
1. DONG HOAN TOAN AI Project OS, ke ca cua so trinh duyet va server dang chay.
2. Giai nen ZIP ra mot thu muc (khong chay truc tiep ben trong ZIP).
3. Bam dup CAI-BAN-VA-3D.cmd. Script tim thu muc cai dat; neu khong tim thay, cho phep chon thu muc.
4. Script chi cap nhat public/tabs/team.js va them public/office-motion.mjs. Script kiem tra hash ma nguon goc dung v0.9.60 de tranh de len phien ban khac.
5. Mo lai chuong trinh > Idea Studio. Phong WebGL 3D bat mac dinh, co the chuyen thu cong ve ISO.
6. De thu di/chuyen ban, su dung tinh nang chon robot va chon ban/vi tri san trong phong 3D.

NOI DUNG SUA:
- Bat WebGL 3D theo mac dinh; giu isometric khi dung chon hoac 3D khong the khoi dong.
- Gan TAM19 GLB cho tat ca robot va robot moi ket noi; khong ve hai than chong len nhau.
- Them AnimationMixer clip ngoi xuong, ngoi cho, ngoi lam viec, dung day tren cac khop co san cua TAM19.
- Dung dung diem ghe, xoay nguoi, di theo waypoint tinh boi A* tranh ban, co chuyen huong va giam toc.
- Khong thay doi business logic, API, du lieu ca nhan, thu muc data hay cau hinh ket noi.

HAN CHE KIEM THU:
- Da pass 38/38 test phong AI va npm run check tren source v0.9.60.
- Chua kiem thu chay EXE Windows va chuyen dong WebGL tren GPU thuc; ban va duoc giao de kiem thu thuc dia.
- Khong tuyet doi hoa do tu nhien: chua co IK chan/bam san, chua collision dong giua robot, chua mo phong keo ghe.
- Chromium tu dong trong moi truong xay dung bi chan truy cap localhost; chua co screenshot xac nhan.

AN TOAN:
- Script backup team.js cu duoi %%LOCALAPPDATA%%\AIProjectOSLocal\backups\idea3d-<thoi-gian>.
- Khong xoa cai dat, tai khoan, token, profile, du lieu hoac file nguoi dung.
- Neu team.js khac hash ban goc 0.9.60, script tu dung, khong de len.
